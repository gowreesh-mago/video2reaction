How to interpret this explorer · model and data details

The visual model maps DINOv2 frame features through a temporal Transformer into three VAD coordinates per scene. Fixed NRC VAD emotion coordinates convert distances to 21 reaction probabilities. Learned relevance and scene duration determine the mixture weights.

scene_probability(c) = softmax(−squared_distance_to_emotion(c) / temperature)
video_probability(c) = Σ scene_weight(t) × scene_probability(t, c)
  • The 3D axes are the model's actual VAD outputs, not a principal-component projection. Fixed reaction coordinates are from NRC VAD Lexicon v2.1.
  • These scene estimates use full-video context. Playback and the running mixture are explanations of a completed prediction, not streaming or causal inference.
  • There is no scene-level emotion ground truth here. Audience targets are whole-video reaction distributions; the plot cannot establish that a particular scene caused a comment.
  • Lines connect successive scenes for orientation. The recorded model uses one constant estimate per scene; smooth motion between points is not observed.
  • Common references are per-video averages of audience label distributions, not pooled comment counts. The selected ten clips are deliberately biased toward diagnostic examples.
  • KL divergence measures distribution mismatch. Zero means identical distributions; larger values are worse. VAD means valence, arousal and dominance. DINO is the visual-feature baseline used in our previous experiments.
